Updating is now one click: Sheer downloads the new version, checks it's genuine, swaps it in and reopens, and your permissions stay on. This one last time, update by hand and turn Sheer on again in the permissions window (from now on, releases are signed with Sheer's own certificate).
---
更新现在只要点一下：Sheer 会自己下载新版本，确认是正版后替换并重新打开，授权也会保留。只有这一次还需要手动更新，并在弹出的授权窗口里重新打开开关（从这一版起，发布的版本都用 Sheer 自己的证书签名）。
---
Unzip, move Sheer.app to Applications, then open it. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
