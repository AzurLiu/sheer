Sheer now also runs on macOS 11 Big Sur (and early Monterey before 12.3), so older Macs like the 11" MacBook Air can try it. Like macOS 12 and 13 support, this is experimental and hasn't been tested on those systems yet; if something looks off, please open an issue with your macOS version and a screenshot of the menu bar. Nothing changes on macOS 14 and 15.
---
Sheer 现在也能在 macOS 11 Big Sur（以及 12.3 之前的早期 Monterey）上运行，11 寸 MacBook Air 这样的老 Mac 也能试了。和 macOS 12、13 一样，这是实验性支持，还没在这些系统上实测过；如果显示不对，请提交 issue，附上系统版本和菜单栏截图。macOS 14 和 15 上没有任何变化。
---
Open Sheer.dmg and drag Sheer onto Applications, then open it. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
