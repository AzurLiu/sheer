Sheer now comes as a disk image too: open Sheer.dmg and drag Sheer onto Applications. The app icon also looks cleaner at small sizes, without the faint grey square around it.
---
Sheer 现在也提供磁盘映像：打开 Sheer.dmg，把 Sheer 拖到「应用程序」就装好了。App 图标在小尺寸下也更干净了，周围不再有一圈淡灰色的方框。
---
Open Sheer.dmg and drag Sheer onto Applications, then open it. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
