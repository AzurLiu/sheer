With a Dynamic wallpaper, Sheer captures the screen far less often: about 80 minutes a day around the wallpaper's changes, instead of about 6 hours, so the screen recording indicator stays off most of the time.
---
使用动态壁纸时，Sheer 截屏的次数大幅减少：只在壁纸换图前后看，每天约 80 分钟，以前约 6 小时，所以录屏指示大部分时间都不会亮。
---
Open Sheer.dmg and drag Sheer onto Applications, then open it. The first time, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
